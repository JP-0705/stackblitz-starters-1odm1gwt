Innovphil Asset Portal

Inventory Management System

This system serves as the official digital record of all Innovphil properties and inventories — appliances, furniture & fixtures, office supplies, IT equipment, vehicles, and AHA equipment — across the Naga and Makati branches. Every item's purchase history, current custodian, condition, and physical count records are maintained here to support accurate asset tracking, accounting, and audits.
